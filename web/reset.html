Reset ZAFAR on this phone

ZAFAR no longer keeps a copy of the app on your phone. An older version left one behind, and this removes it — not your account, not your downloads, not your favourites.

If this page will not load

Then an older copy is intercepting the page as well, and only the browser can clear it. The menu names move between versions, but the setting is the same one:

  • Chrome on Android: ⋮ → Settings → Site settings → All sites → find this site → Clear & reset. Or tap the padlock in the address bar → Cookies and site data → Manage.
  • Safari on iPhone: Settings app → Safari → Advanced → Website Data → find this site → Delete. (Settings → Apps → Safari on newer versions.)
  • Firefox on Android: tap the site's padlock → Site settings → Clear data.
  • Any browser: close every ZAFAR tab before and after — a tab still open can put the old copy back.

Clearing site data signs you out. Nothing else is lost — your account and your downloads are on the server, and favourites are stored with the site data, so save anything you want to keep.
