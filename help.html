How to download a paper

  1. Open Past Papers from the sidebar.
  2. Use the search bar, or the sidebar's MSCE / JCE / Mock / Other Past Papers links, to find what you need.
  3. Tap the Download button on the paper's card.
  4. The file saves straight to your phone's Downloads folder — no login to a viewer needed.
  5. Each account has a daily download limit; the badge at the top of the page shows how many are left today.
